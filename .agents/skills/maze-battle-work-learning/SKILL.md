# Maze Battle design and development learning

Use the installed `work-learning` skill/shared helper with scope `maze-battle`.
This is a repository-owned domain router, not a new gameplay memory system.
Read the relevant [source map](references/source-map.md), and keep
[maze-world-geometry](../maze-world-geometry/SKILL.md) and
[maze-visual-language](../maze-visual-language/SKILL.md) as the procedure owners.

Before reconstructing a substantial design or repeating a spatial correction,
retrieve at most three useful global-plus-domain records from the owned checkout:

```powershell
python "$env:USERPROFILE/.agents/skills/work-learning/scripts/learning_store.py" --repo . search --scope maze-battle --query "usable room clearance consumer admission"
```

Recheck source/placement pins before relying on a lesson. The local cell lattice,
renderer units and Tile Lab's metric stock are different contracts; apply the
declared conversion once. Net usable room and circulation space must consume
walls, structure, stairs, furniture and operating clearances. A mesh or import
receipt cannot authorize movement, reveal rooms or change canonical saves.

Capture a material correction or tested reusable finding through the shared
record contract. Accepted domain records belong in `.agents/learning/records`
via `--repo . record --local --file <lesson.json>`; publish the identical record
to the shared `maze-battle` inbox for cross-worktree discovery. Append corrected
records with `supersedes`, preserving attribution and applicability. Keep Tile Lab
implementation facts in its own scope; use an explicit cross-domain lookup when
the current integration actually needs them. Do not copy participant/game memory,
saves, secrets or full transcripts into the engineering ledger.

Keep unfinished work in its owning goal or shared `checkpoint`/`resume` capsule,
with stable work ID, actual acceptance boundary, cumulative budget reference,
uncertain effects and next action. Continue the authorized producer-to-consumer
work through the requested acceptance; local preview success is a checkpoint.
This skill grants no asset installation, migration, provider spending or merge.

If shared storage is unavailable, use relevant local records and a bounded
pending record in the owned workspace; continue authorized work and reconcile
later. Do not replace the shared storage logic or mutate another worker's checkout.
